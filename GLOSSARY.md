# Stefan's blog

A personal website where Stefan writes down what he learns while building AI platforms. It exists to build his personal brand while staying down to earth.

## Content

**Post**:
A dated piece of writing by Stefan, published on the blog. A Post either belongs to a Series or stands alone as something interesting he found on the net.
_Avoid_: Article, entry, deep dive

**Talk**:
A presentation Stefan gives at a conference or meetup, listed with its abstract, recording and resources.
_Avoid_: Presentation, session

**Deck**:
The slides Stefan shows during a Talk.
_Avoid_: Presentation, slides (fine casually)

## Classification

**Series**:
A recurring monthly format that a Post belongs to.
_Avoid_: Category, column

**Topic**:
A subject a Post or Talk is about, independent of any Series.
_Avoid_: Category, tag

## Series

**TIL** (This month I learned):
A monthly Series collecting what Stefan learned that month.

**FPE** (Favorite podcast episodes):
A monthly Series recommending podcast episodes Stefan enjoyed.
_Avoid_: Podcast picks (fine as homepage wording, not as the name)

**CMT** (Closing my tabs):
A monthly Series in which Stefan reviews the browser tabs he had open and writes about the most interesting ones.

## Design

**Brand**:
The colours and fonts that the site and Stefan's personal Decks share.
_Avoid_: Theme, design system, look
